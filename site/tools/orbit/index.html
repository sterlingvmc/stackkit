Orbit

AnalyticsBuilt for Substack
Visit Orbit ↗

Our verdict: A thoughtful relationship tracker for Substack that never writes or replies for you, and it connects to Claude. It works through a Chrome extension running alongside your logged-in Substack session (DMs off by default), and there's no way to try it before paying.

Best for

Writers growing through relationships who want to remember who they've talked to across Notes, comments and DMs

Pricing

$12/mo or $120/yr, included with a paid subscription to creator Rebecca Spitzer's Substack. No free plan or trial.
