Our verdict: Promising revenue-first analytics built specifically for Substack. It is a beta product; live sync uses your Substack session cookie and undocumented internal APIs, so the CSV-upload mode is the safer start.

Best for

Paid-newsletter writers who want MRR, churn, cohort and revenue-per-post analytics

Pricing

Starter free (CSV import, 30-day retention); Growth $19/mo; Scale $49/mo. 14-day trial. Currently in private beta.
