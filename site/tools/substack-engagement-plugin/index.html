Substack Engagement Plugin

Growth & NotesBuilt for Substack
Visit Substack Engagement Plugin ↗

Our verdict: A plugin for Claude or ChatGPT desktop that drafts replies to your comments, comments on other writers' Notes, Notes of your own, restack lines and recommendation-swap pitches, plus a weekly scorecard ranked by subscribers. Every draft waits in an approval list; if you choose, it posts approved drafts one at a time up to a daily limit, and it never sends cold DMs. The upside is speed; the risk is sounding like everyone else using AI, so edit before you approve. The maker's own results (replies up 212% in a month) are self-reported and, as she says, not typical. It works best with the Claude in Chrome extension or ChatGPT's browser tools reading your Substack; otherwise you paste or screenshot.

Best for

Writers who know commenting and replying grows their list but can't give it an hour a day, and want AI drafts in their own voice that they approve one by one

Pricing

$67 one-time for the Claude and ChatGPT versions, with updates included. A $97 bundle adds the maker's 35-page Substack Engagement Playbook. No free plan or trial listed.
