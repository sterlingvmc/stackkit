NotesIQ

AnalyticsBuilt for Substack
Visit NotesIQ ↗

Our verdict: The cheapest Notes analytics extension we've found, and it keeps data in your browser with no account. Very new (v1.0.3, launched Sept 2026, no Chrome Web Store ratings yet) from a solo developer, and its price has already changed since launch, so treat it as an inexpensive experiment. Scheduling publishes through Substack's own composer in your logged-in session.

Best for

Writers who want a cheap, one-time-payment Notes dashboard with a best-time heatmap and a weekly queue

Pricing

$4.99 one-time for lifetime access (analytics, scheduler, competitor lookups); 7-day money-back guarantee.

Source: official site (notesiq.app pricing section and FAQ; matches its Chrome Web Store listing)
