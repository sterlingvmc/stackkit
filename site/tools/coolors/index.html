Coolors

DesignFree plan
Visit Coolors ↗

Our verdict: A fast, free way to stop re-deciding colors every week: generate or browse a palette, then reuse the hex codes in Canva, Kittl or Substack's new website editor. The free plan is enough for one newsletter brand; Pro is mostly about removing ads and saving more.

Best for

Picking one consistent color palette for headers, Notes images and your site

Pricing

Free forever (with ads): palettes up to 5 colors, 10K+ ready color schemes, save up to 10 palettes, 1 project and 1 collection. Pro from $3/mo (per Coolors' own upgrade prompt) adds no ads, up to 10 colors, unlimited saves, palette contrast checks and Coolors AI (3,000 credits/mo).

Source: official site (coolors.co/pricing plan features and FAQ; the card's monthly price renders client-side, so the Pro price is from the page's own '$3/mo' upgrade prompt; third-party trackers list $3/mo billed yearly)
