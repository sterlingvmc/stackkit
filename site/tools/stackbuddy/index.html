StackBuddy

Growth & NotesFree planBuilt for Substack
Visit StackBuddy ↗

Our verdict: A Notes scheduler with a free plan that keeps scheduling, and its Chrome extension hands each Note to Substack's own scheduler, so Notes publish from Substack's servers with your computer off. Pro adds voice-matching AI drafts, analytics and LinkedIn cross-posting. Small one-developer tool; the extension acts in your logged-in Substack session and syncs your existing Notes to StackBuddy's dashboard, so read its privacy page first.

Best for

Batch-scheduling Notes into posting slots and finding other writers to cross-promote with

Pricing

Free plan (scheduling and creator network free forever); Pro $12.50/mo billed yearly or $15/mo month-to-month. Every account starts with 14 days of Pro, no card.

Source: official site (stackbuddy.io FAQ and pricing section) + stackbuddy.io/terms and /privacy; Chrome Web Store listing (offered by Patrick God)
