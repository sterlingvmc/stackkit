Excalidraw

DesignFree plan
Visit Excalidraw ↗

Our verdict: The fastest way to turn a paragraph of explanation into a simple diagram: draw boxes and arrows, export a PNG, and add it to your post like any image. The free web app saves drawings in your browser's storage, which can be cleared, so save a file of anything you'll want to edit later.

Best for

Quick hand-drawn-style diagrams and process sketches to drop into posts

Pricing

Free and open source (MIT license); optional Excalidraw+ at $6/user/mo with a 14-day free trial.

Source: official site (plus.excalidraw.com/pricing) + github.com/excalidraw/excalidraw README
