Ulysses

Editing & Proofreading
Visit Ulysses ↗

Our verdict: A polished Markdown editor with a library that syncs across Mac, iPad and iPhone, writing goals, and a built-in grammar and style check in 20+ languages. Since Nov 2025 it can copy a draft formatted for Substack (Quick Export, Services, Substack), keeping most formatting and footnotes; tables and videos become placeholder images and only images with URLs come across, so check the paste in Substack's editor. Direct publishing is for WordPress, Ghost and Micro.blog (its home page also lists Medium), not Substack. No Windows or Android version, and it's a subscription, so iA Writer's one-time price is cheaper over a few years.

Best for

Apple users who want one writing app for drafts, long projects and copy-ready Substack posts

Pricing

Subscription only: $39.99/yr or $5.99/mo (U.S. prices; local App Store prices may differ). Free, fully functional trial; reduced pricing for students. Mac, iPad and iPhone only.

Source: official site (ulysses.app/pricing, U.S. prices, checked 2026-11-25) + ulysses.app home page (free trial on all devices; publishing to WordPress, Ghost, Medium, Micro.blog) + Ulysses Help 'Quick Export' (Publishing to Substack and Basecamp) + Mac App Store listing (requires macOS 15.4 or later; copy-paste publishing to Substack)
