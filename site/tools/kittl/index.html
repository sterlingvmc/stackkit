Kittl

DesignFree plan
Visit Kittl ↗

Our verdict: A stylish Canva alternative with great type tools. Kittl's site is inconsistent on the free plan's license: the pricing FAQ says free is for personal use only (no commercial rights), while the live Free card lists a 'Full commercial license'. Check Kittl's license terms before any commercial use; if your Substack earns money, assume you need Pro (which includes a commercial license) until Kittl clarifies.

Best for

Typography-heavy graphics and AI-assisted illustrations

Pricing

Free plan; Pro $19/mo or $168/yr ($14/mo billed yearly); Expert $49/mo or $468/yr ($39/mo billed yearly); Max $85/mo or $780/yr ($65/mo billed yearly) (USD, US prices as shown on Kittl's live pricing cards). Prices vary by country; confirm at checkout.

Source: official site (kittl.com/pricing live cards for US visitors, and the FAQ license section, re-checked 2026-11-13)
