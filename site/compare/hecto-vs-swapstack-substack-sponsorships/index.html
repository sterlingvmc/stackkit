Hecto vs Swapstack for Substack sponsorships

Checked 2026-11-13 on the vendors' sites and Substack's help center · Independent, no affiliate relationship with either tool

The short answer: Pick Hecto if you want to sell your own sponsorship slots at prices you set, with a checkout link and a 5% fee only when a slot sells. Pick Swapstack if you'd rather browse ready-made affiliate and pay-per-click deals and take invitations from brands; it's free for publishers, with a 10% cut on invite-only campaigns. Both are free to join, so many writers use both. Paved only opens up once you reach 50k subscribers.

Where Paved fits

Paved calls itself the world's largest newsletter ad network, but its publisher page sets minimums: 50k subscribers for flat-fee scheduled ads and 1M monthly impressions for pay-per-click native ads. Most Substacks should start with Hecto or Swapstack and revisit Paved once they clear those numbers.

Price your slot before you list it

Whichever platform you use, decide your rate first. A common starting point in newsletter advertising is a price per thousand opens, so work from your real open count in Substack's dashboard rather than your total subscriber number. Then put a simple sponsor page on your Substack linking to your Hecto checkout or media kit.

Keep reader trust

Only take sponsors you'd recommend anyway, label the placement clearly as sponsored, and keep it to one slot per post. A sponsor that doesn't fit your readers costs you more in unsubscribes than it pays.

Our pick

For most Substack writers under 50k subscribers, start with Hecto: you control the price and only pay 5% when a slot sells. Add Swapstack to see which affiliate and pay-per-click deals match your niche, and treat both as free experiments until a sponsor actually books.

Our verdicts

  • Hecto: A straightforward sponsorship marketplace: you publish ad packages with upfront prices and a checkout link. Listing is free, but demand varies by niche. Main site and pricing page were offline with a hosting error on Oct 31, 2026; both returned HTTP 200 again on Nov 6, 2026 with the same 5% / £0 monthly fee.
  • Swapstack: One of the original newsletter sponsorship marketplaces, with flat-rate sponsorships, pay-per-click and affiliate deals in one place. Worth a free signup to see what matches your niche. Much of its public site still shows 2022-era content and the paid plans have been 'coming soon' for a long time, so judge it by the deals you're actually offered.
