iA Writer vs Ulysses for drafting Substack posts

Checked 2026-11-25 on the vendors' sites and Substack's help center · Independent, no affiliate relationship with either tool

The short answer: Pick iA Writer if you want to pay once ($49.99 Mac, $39.99 Windows, $49.99 iPhone and iPad) and like a bare, focused page, including on Windows. Pick Ulysses ($39.99/yr or $5.99/mo, Apple devices only) if you want one synced library for every draft and a built-in Copy for Substack option that keeps most formatting and footnotes when you paste. Neither publishes straight to Substack.

Do you need either?

Substack's own editor and Google Docs are free and work fine for drafting. A writing app earns its price when you write most days and want a calm place to draft, keep every post and idea in one library, and stop losing drafts across tabs. If that's not you, skip both.

Paying once vs paying yearly

iA Writer costs $49.99 once on a Mac, while Ulysses costs $39.99 every year, so iA Writer is cheaper from the second year on if you write on one platform. If you use a Mac and an iPhone, iA Writer is two purchases ($99.98) against one Ulysses subscription that covers all your Apple devices. iA says major updates may cost extra, so a one-time purchase isn't lifetime.

Moving a draft into Substack

This is the practical difference for Substack writers. Ulysses added a Copy for Substack option in late 2025: open Quick Export (Command-6 on Mac), choose Services, then Substack, and paste into a new Substack post. Most formatting and footnotes carry over, but add images, tables and videos in Substack's editor. With iA Writer you copy the text across yourself and fix headings, links and images in Substack. Either way, preview the post and send yourself a test email before publishing.

Windows or Apple

Ulysses is Apple-only. If you write on a Windows PC, iA Writer is the one of these two that works for you.

Our pick

Windows users and anyone who hates subscriptions: iA Writer. Apple users who draft on several devices and want the cleanest paste into Substack: Ulysses, starting with its free trial.

Our verdicts

  • iA Writer: A calm, well-made Markdown editor: Focus Mode fades everything but the sentence you're on, and Style Check flags fillers and clichés on your device without rewriting anything for you. You pay once instead of monthly, but per platform, and iA says major updates may cost extra. On Mac it can publish straight to WordPress, Ghost and Micro.blog, but not to Substack, so you'll still copy each draft into Substack's editor and check the formatting there. Free tools like Google Docs do the drafting job if you don't care about the writing environment.
  • Ulysses: A polished Markdown editor with a library that syncs across Mac, iPad and iPhone, writing goals, and a built-in grammar and style check in 20+ languages. Since Nov 2025 it can copy a draft formatted for Substack (Quick Export, Services, Substack), keeping most formatting and footnotes; tables and videos become placeholder images and only images with URLs come across, so check the paste in Substack's editor. Direct publishing is for WordPress, Ghost and Micro.blog (its home page also lists Medium), not Substack. No Windows or Android version, and it's a subscription, so iA Writer's one-time price is cheaper over a few years.
