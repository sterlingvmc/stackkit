Buffer vs Hypefury for Substack writers

Checked 2026-11-11 on the vendors' sites and Substack's help center · Independent, no affiliate relationship with either tool

The short answer: Pick Buffer if you want one calendar for Substack Notes plus X and your other networks: it supports Substack as a channel and has a free plan for up to 3 channels. Pick Hypefury if you post to Instagram, Threads, LinkedIn, TikTok, Facebook or Bluesky and want automation like Instagram auto-DMs and auto first comments, but note it no longer supports X or Notes.

The deciding question: where do you promote your Substack?

If Substack Notes and X are where your readers find you, Buffer is the only one of the two that covers both. Hypefury dropped X and never added Notes, so it only makes sense if your promotion happens on Instagram, Threads, LinkedIn, TikTok, Facebook or Bluesky.

How the pricing compares

Buffer charges per channel, so it's cheapest when you use one to three networks, and the free plan may be all you need. Hypefury's Creator plan is a flat $19/mo for all six of its platforms, which its own pricing page says beats paying per platform once you use four or more.

Keep your voice

Both tools can generate posts with AI. Whatever you pick, write the promotion for each post yourself, or at least edit it hard. Readers follow a Substack writer for their voice, and generic AI teasers tend to get scrolled past.

Our pick

For most Substack writers, Buffer: it schedules Notes, still posts to X, and starts free. Choose Hypefury if Instagram or LinkedIn is your main channel and you want its automation, and keep scheduling Notes in Substack's built-in scheduler.

Our verdicts

  • Buffer: Reliable and affordable, and its free plan includes Substack Notes scheduling. The best default if you post to several networks.
  • Hypefury: Hypefury no longer supports X, so it's now a scheduler for six platforms (Instagram, Facebook, Threads, LinkedIn, TikTok and Bluesky), with auto first comments (Autoplugs), Instagram auto-DMs and an engagement builder. Creator is the cheapest flat price once you post to 4 or more platforms. Substack Notes isn't on its platform list, so pair it with Substack's own scheduler.
