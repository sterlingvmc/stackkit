Buffer vs Typefully for scheduling Substack Notes

Checked 2026-10-26 on the vendors' sites and Substack's help center · Independent, no affiliate relationship with either tool

The short answer: Both free plans schedule Substack Notes, so you can try either at no cost. Pick Buffer if Notes are one channel among many (including Instagram, TikTok or Facebook) or you want to see which Notes brought subscribers. Pick Typefully if you write for X or LinkedIn first and want a writing-focused editor. If you only post Notes, Substack's free built-in scheduler may be all you need.

Start with the free plans

Unlike most Substack-only Notes tools, both of these include Substack Notes on their free plans. Buffer's free plan caps how many posts can be waiting (10 per channel at once), while Typefully's caps how many you publish in a month (10). If you post a Note a day, Buffer's free plan keeps working as long as you keep your queue short; Typefully's free plan runs out after about ten days.

How the costs add up

Buffer charges per channel, so Substack plus LinkedIn on Essentials is about $10/mo billed yearly. Typefully's Creator plan is a flat $99 a year (about $8.25/mo) for one social set. Compare the two at the number of channels you actually use: Buffer's cost grows with every channel you add, Typefully's Creator price doesn't.

Neither replaces replying

A scheduler helps you show up consistently, but much of the growth on Notes comes from replying to other writers, which you still do in Substack itself. Treat either tool as a way to free up time for that.

Our pick

For most Substack writers who also post elsewhere, Buffer: its free plan covers three channels and it reports subscribers per Note. Choose Typefully if X or LinkedIn is your main channel and you already like writing there. For every other option, including Substack-only batch schedulers, see our Substack Notes schedulers comparison.

Our verdicts

  • Buffer: Reliable and affordable, and its free plan includes Substack Notes scheduling. The best default if you post to several networks.
  • Typefully: A polished writing-first scheduler that now publishes and schedules Substack Notes on every plan, including free. It handles Notes only (no long-form posts, no video in Notes), and its paid analytics are for X, so it suits writers who start on X or LinkedIn more than Notes-first writers.
