Gumroad vs Ko-fi for Substack writers

Checked 2026-11-08 on the vendors' sites and Substack's help center · Independent, no affiliate relationship with either tool

The short answer: Pick Ko-fi if you mainly want a tip jar next to your Substack: one-off tips are fee-free on Ko-fi Free (you pay only PayPal or Stripe fees). Pick Gumroad if you're selling an ebook, template or guide and want sales tax handled for you; it costs more per sale but acts as merchant of record.

How each fits next to a Substack

Substack already handles paid subscriptions, so neither tool should replace that. Ko-fi works as the low-commitment option for readers who want to say thanks without subscribing. Gumroad works for a one-time product, like a guide built from your best posts, that you link at the bottom of a newsletter.

Do the fee math on a real price

On a $10 ebook sold through a direct link, Gumroad's fee is $1.50 (10% + $0.50) and covers payment processing. On Ko-fi Shop the service fee is $0.50 (5%), plus your PayPal or Stripe fee, often around $0.60 on $10. Ko-fi usually keeps more of each sale; Gumroad's extra cost buys sales tax handling and a buyer checkout many readers already know.

Watch Ko-fi's default mode

Ko-fi says new creators now start with everything unlocked, which means a 5% fee on one-time tips too. If tips are all you need, turn Standard mode off in Settings > Payment and tips go back to 0% service fees.

Our pick

Tip jar only: Ko-fi Free. Selling one or two digital products and you don't want to think about tax: Gumroad. Plenty of writers use both, Ko-fi for thanks and Gumroad for the product.

Our verdicts

  • Gumroad: The simplest way to sell a digital product to your readers. Fees are high per sale but there's no fixed cost, and Gumroad acts as merchant of record, collecting and remitting sales tax for you.
  • Ko-fi: A low-friction tip jar that complements paid subscriptions. One-off tips are fee-free on Ko-fi Free, but new accounts start in Standard mode (5% on tips), so switch it off in Settings > Payment if tips are all you need.
