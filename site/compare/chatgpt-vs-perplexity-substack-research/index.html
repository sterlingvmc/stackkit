ChatGPT vs Perplexity for Substack research

Checked 2026-10-29 on the vendors' sites and Substack's help center · Independent, no affiliate relationship with either tool

The short answer: Pick Perplexity when you need facts you can check: every answer comes with citations, and the free plan covers light research. Pick ChatGPT when you want a brainstorming and editing partner for angles, outlines and headlines; its free plan includes unlimited everyday text chats, Go is $8/mo and Plus is $20/mo. Perplexity Pro is $20/mo, or $17/mo billed annually.

Use each for what it's good at

Most Substack writers get the most out of these as two different tools. Perplexity is for questions with a factual answer: a statistic, a date, what a study actually found. ChatGPT is for the thinking around the post: ten possible angles, a tighter outline, five headline options, or a blunt read on a draft.

Always check the facts

Readers trust a newsletter that gets details right. Perplexity puts citations in every answer, which makes checking quick, but open the source and read the line yourself before you quote it. Treat any fact from ChatGPT the same way, and look it up before it goes in a post.

Keep your voice

Neither tool should write the post for you. Readers subscribe for how you think and write, and AI-drafted prose tends to sound like everyone else's. Use them for research and structure, then write in your own words.

How the costs compare

Both free plans are enough to try the workflow. If you pay for one, ChatGPT Go at $8/mo is the cheapest paid step and Plus is $20/mo. Perplexity Pro is also $20/mo, or $17/mo if you pay for a year up front. ChatGPT's individual plans are billed monthly only.

Our pick

Start with both free plans: Perplexity for fact-finding and ChatGPT for brainstorming and edits. If you only pay for one, pick the job you do most. Writers who publish researched, data-heavy posts get more from Perplexity Pro; writers who mostly need help shaping ideas get more from ChatGPT. For long-draft feedback, also see Claude in the AI Writing & Research category.

Our verdicts

  • ChatGPT: A versatile thinking partner. Use it for ideas and edits rather than finished prose your readers will recognize as generic.
  • Perplexity: Faster than a search engine for background research. Always click through and verify the citations.
