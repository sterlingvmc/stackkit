Design tools for Substack writers

Header images, social cards and brand visuals without hiring a designer.

Check Substack's own site editor first

On Nov 12, 2026 Substack launched new website design tools: seven themes, a Mini-Editor on your publication homepage for layouts, color palettes and fonts, and a Full Editor for your Home, Welcome and Post pages (header, hero, body, footer, custom colors, typefaces and a logo or wordmark image), with desktop, tablet and mobile web previews. Changes don't show in the Substack app, which keeps its own layout. Substack's announcement ↗

So use the built-in editor for your site's look, and the tools below for what it doesn't make: header and preview images, social cards and logos. Sizes and a side-by-side are in Canva vs Kittl for Substack images.

Before you upload, shrink big exports and screenshots with Squoosh (free, runs in your browser) so posts load faster on phones. Every recommended size, thumbnail cropping and alt text are in our Substack images guide.
