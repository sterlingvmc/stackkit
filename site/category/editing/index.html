Your post title is your email subject line

On Substack, the title you type in the editor is what lands in the inbox. The separate SEO title and SEO description in post settings only affect search results: Substack says they don't change how the post appears in email, in the app or on your site, and recommends keeping the SEO title under 60 characters. Substack's SEO guide ↗

If you have at least 200 subscribers, Substack can test titles for you: on the Publish page, scroll to "Run a title test", add alternate titles, and pick a test size and length (Substack suggests 50% of recipients for one hour). The title with the highest open rate is sent to everyone else automatically. Substack Help: title testing ↗

To brainstorm options before a test, Headline Studio scores titles and has a free plan. For the search-facing title, see our Substack SEO guide.
